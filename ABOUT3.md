# Controls



The robot will be operated by one (1) standard Xbox Controller. Below is the correct mapping to functions:

Left Trigger: Intake. Where the button is pressed more than 0.08, scale the power of the gather motor accordingly (where 0.5 = 0.5 power)
Left Bumper: Reverse intake. Full power, but make the power configurable
Left Joystick (x axis): Rotate robot
Right Trigger: Launch, where the button is pressed more than 0.15, begin the launch sequence.
Right Bumper: Jostle, at values specified in constants files
Right Joystick (x and y axes): Translation of our robot
Up Arrow: Begin climbing Procedure
Down Arrow: Toggle Jostle Type (Flat, Absolute Value, Sinusoidal)
